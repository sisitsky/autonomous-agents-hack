# Growth playbook (Loop 3, run through Egbe)

The growth agent rewrites this file after each cycle. Humans approve outreach templates.
Conversion data may change targeting and messaging only, never price.

## Current hypothesis (cycle 0)
- Segment: startups shipping customer-facing chatbots or agents (support, sales, onboarding).
- Offer: free AI risk score (intake + red-team sample + indicative premium); paid full report via Egbe Stripe checkout.
- Channels: landing page + small ad test; founder outreach to companies with public AI products.
- Success metric: risk-score signups per $ spent, then report purchases.

## Limits (from params/guardrails.yaml)
- Daily ad spend, total spend, and daily outreach volume caps apply.
- Every outreach template needs human approval before first send.
